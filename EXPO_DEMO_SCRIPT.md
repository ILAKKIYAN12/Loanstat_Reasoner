# 🎤 Expo Presentation Showcase Script (3-Minute Live Demo)

Use this script during your live Expo presentation to showcase the key capabilities of the 3D XAI Loan Approval System.

---

## 🎬 Stage 1: The Problem & Introduction (30 Seconds)
**Presenter**: *"Black-box AI models make millions of critical financial decisions every day — like loan approvals — without telling applicants WHY. Today, we present our 3D Interactive Explainable AI (XAI) Loan Engine."*

- **Action**: Show the main screen with the floating 3D particle grid. Point out the bilingual language toggle (**English / தமிழ்**) and Preset Profile buttons.

---

## 🔨 Stage 2: 3D Verdict Reveal & Hammer Slam (45 Seconds)
**Presenter**: *"Let's test a Business Owner profile. Watch as our Random Forest model processes income, credit score, loan amount, and debt ratio in real time."*

- **Action**: Click `💼 Business Owner` preset. Click `⚡ Run 3D AI Prediction`.
- **Visual Impact**: 
  - Watch the **3D Judge's Gavel / Hammer slam down** onto the wooden podium!
  - Golden sparks burst out + Green Confetti explodes + Verdict flips to **LOAN APPROVED** (78% probability on the 3D gauge).
- **Audio Feature**: Click `🔊 Hear why`. The Web Speech API speaks the explanation aloud in English or Tamil.

---

## 📊 Stage 3: Transparent SHAP & LIME 3D Explanations (60 Seconds)
**Presenter**: *"Why was this applicant approved? AI transparency means no guesswork. Here is the 3D SHAP Waterfall Chart."*

- **Action**: Click `🔍 Explore 3D SHAP Reason`.
- **Visual Impact**: 
  - Show the 3D extruded bars. Click on the top bar (Credit Score).
  - Point to the human-readable sentence: *"Credit score of 780 boosted approval by 35%."*
  - Toggle between **SHAP (Exact Tree Attribution)** and **LIME (Local Linear Model)**.

---

## 🔄 Stage 4: Counterfactual "What-If" Analysis (30 Seconds)
**Presenter**: *"What if an applicant was rejected? XAI provides actionable guidance."*

- **Action**: Drag the Counterfactual credit score slider down from 780 to 580.
- **Visual Impact**: Watch the verdict live-flip to **LOAN REJECTED** as the 3D Red Stamp slams onto the document with screen shake!

---

## 🖥️ Stage 5: Auto-Kiosk Showcase Mode (15 Seconds)
**Presenter**: *"For exhibition booths, the system features a 20-second idle auto-play kiosk mode that continuously cycles through applicant profiles hands-free."*

- **Action**: Step away from the controls for 20 seconds to demonstrate automatic continuous showcase playback.
